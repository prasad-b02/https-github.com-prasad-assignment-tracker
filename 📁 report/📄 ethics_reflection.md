# AI Ethics & Responsible Development Reflection

## 🤖 Responsible AI Implementation

In developing the **AI Priority Classifier** for the Assignment Tracker, key ethical principles were integrated into the algorithm's design:

---

## 🛡️ Core Ethical Considerations

1. **Algorithmic Fairness:** Priority scoring treats all subject assignments neutrally using standard mathematical formulas, ensuring unbiased prioritization.
2. **Data Transparency:** The mathematical criteria (days remaining, workload, subject weight) are fully visible to the student.
3. **Data Privacy:** All task entries and personal schedules are stored locally in `assignments.db` without exporting personal data to third parties.
4. **User Agency:** The AI provides recommendations and urgency tags, but final scheduling decisions remain entirely under human control.
